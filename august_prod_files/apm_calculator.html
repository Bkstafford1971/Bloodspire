Experimental balance idea, not live in the game. Weapon's own speed sets the starting APM instead of a flat 3.0; STR/DEX/INT each have a "normal" range with no bonus, and only reward/penalize outside it; activity is neutral at 5 instead of always being a bonus.
Same as Dead-Zone Design, but DEX and INT contribute nothing to APM at all - the idea being they'd instead feed a separate accuracy mechanic (not modeled here). STR dead zone, weapon base, activity, race, and style still apply exactly as in Dead-Zone Design.
