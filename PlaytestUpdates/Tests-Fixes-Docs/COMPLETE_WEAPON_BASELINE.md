## Key Principles Applied

1. **Weight determines damage potential**
   - Light weapons (0.5-3.5 wt): 6-11 baseline damage
   - Medium weapons (3.8-5.5 wt): 15-26 baseline damage
   - Heavy weapons (6.0-7.5 wt): 26-34 baseline damage

2. **APM compensates for lower damage**
   - Fast weapons (APM 6-7) attack frequently, do less per hit
   - Slow weapons (APM 3) attack rarely, do more per hit
   - DPS balances out across all weapons

3. **Similar weapons scale similarly**
   - All swords follow same progression
   - All axes follow same progression
   - etc.

4. **Special weapons adjusted for role**
   - Shields do less damage (defensive tool)
   - Martial arts weapons competitive
   - Control weapons (net, bola) do standard damage
