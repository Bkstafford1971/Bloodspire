================================================================================
INTERPRETING OUTPUT
================================================================================

Win Rate: Percentage of fights won by the test warrior
  - 50%+ means trait provides meaningful advantage
  - 40-50% means trait is balanced
  - Below 40% means trait is offset by scenario disadvantages

Trigger Rate (Frenzy): Percentage of fights where frenzy activates
  - 30-60% is expected (depends on damage dealt)
  - Higher = more consistent activation
  - Lower = may indicate threshold issues

Ground Recovery: Recovery messages counted from narrative
  - 1+ per 20 fights means flavor is active
  - Includes "springs lightly" (Tabaxi-specific) and other recovery lines

APM (Attacks Per Minute): Direct measurement from _calc_apm()
  - Tabaxi APM >= Human APM = spear exception working
  - Tabaxi APM < Human APM = potential issue with wrapper

================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

Q: Frenzy not triggering enough?
A: Increase damage in scenario (use stronger opponents). Frenzy requires
   HP to drop to <=30%. Check that opponent deals >10 damage per attack.

Q: Spear exception APM not matching?
A: Verify spear_exception=True in races.py:383. Run test with STR 7
   warriors. Small differences (1 APM) are within RNG range.

Q: Knockdown resistance not visible?
A: Run 100+ knockdown tests for statistical significance. Run both
   test_tabaxi_traits.py (unit test) and test_tabaxi_acrobatic.py (scenario).

Q: Flavor lines missing?
A: Check narrative output in full. Look for "springs lightly", "frenzy",
   "primal fury", etc. Some fights may not trigger ground recovery.
