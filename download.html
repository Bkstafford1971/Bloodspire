Installation & First Launch

Windows Instructions

The official installer automates the setup so you can begin your ascent immediately.

Download the BloodspireArena.exe and run it. The installer will create the necessary folders and place a shortcut on your desktop automatically.

macOS Instructions

Follow these steps to authorize the client for the arena:

  1. Double-click the .zip to extract bloodspire.app.
  2. Drag bloodspire.app into /Applications.
  3. First launch only: Right-click bloodspire in Applications → click Open → click Open again in the dialog.
  4. After that, normal double-clicking will work.
Note: The macOS right-click method is required to bypass Gatekeeper for unsigned applications.

League Connectivity & Support

To join official matches and compete against other managers, ensure your client is connected to the arena network.

  1. Server URL: The official arena server is located at:
    http://bloodspirearena.dpdns.org
  2. Technical Support: For any issues, contact the Admin: Ilneval at Ilneval@gmail.com. Please include your Manager Name and a description of the issue you are experiencing.
Note: Progress, gladiator stats, and league standing are updated in real-time while connected to the server.
