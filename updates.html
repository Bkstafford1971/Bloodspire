System Updates & Bug Fixes

Latest stability improvements, gameplay adjustments, and engine fixes.

v1.1.0 Changelog

Combat & Gameplay Balance

  • Finesse Weapon Scaling Adjusted damage calculations for finesse weapons. Previously, finesse weapons were too ineffective in combat scales compared to strength-based gear. They now scale appropriately to match alternative playstyles.
  • Blood Challenge Logic Updated and refined the triggers and scaling backend for Blood Challenges to ensure they execute consistently.
  • Challenge Selection Save State Fixed a critical interface bug where selected challenges failed to commit to saving. Selected challenge details are now correctly retained in local storage.

Interface & User Experience

  • Record Privacy Option Added the ability to hide your Win-Loss-Kill (W-L-K) record, accessible directly under the Preferences tab.
  • Newsletter Subscription Form Clean-up Removed the "voice" dropdown menu from the newsletter tab to streamline the interface.
  • Arena Rebranding Formally updated all references of the primary combat grounds to The Agony Amphitheatre.

System Compatibility & Web Standards

  • Multi-Browser Compatibility Support Expanded engine rendering configurations. Previously, Google Chrome was designated as the hardcoded default rendering platform, causing structural and display errors on workstations running alternative browsers.
  • Microsoft Edge Rendering Alignment Resolved structural restrictions where security settings native to Microsoft Edge blocked team data from rendering properly. Team displays now align seamlessly across Edge and Safari platforms.
