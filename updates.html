System Updates & Bug Fixes

Latest stability improvements, gameplay adjustments, and engine fixes.

v2.7.4 Changelog
June 7, 2026

Combat Narrative & Flow Fixes

  • Counter-Attack Weapon Errors Fixed a confusing bug where a warrior who successfully parried an attack and executed an immediate counter-strike would be described as hitting the enemy with the opponent's weapon instead of their own. Narratives now properly display the defender's weapon during a riposte.
  • Simultaneous Strategy Shifts When two combatants changed their combat tactics at the exact same moment due to a major damage event, the text engine would split the descriptions up, showing other atmospheric text in between. These strategy changes are now displayed back-to-back as they happen.
  • Counter-Attack Strategy Updates Resolved an oversight where the warrior initiating a counter-attack would not instantly evaluate tactical options based on the target's newly reduced health. Both fighters now dynamically adjust strategies instantly after counter-strike damage.
  • Armor-Specific Narrative Inconsistencies Combat flavor text will no longer describe an attacker finding gaps in "iron plates" when fighting an opponent wearing soft leather armor. Descriptions are now fully categorized to match the exact armor class the opponent is wearing.
  • Unmistakable Challenge Types Challenge declarations have been completely rewritten to explicitly name the type of match occurring. It is now completely transparent whether you are witnessing a Standard Challenge, a Blood Challenge, a Monster Challenge, or a Title Challenge.
  • Missing Thrown Weapon Results Fixed an issue where thrown weapon attacks that were cleanly parried or missed entirely would omit their results from the battle log, leaving players confused. All thrown actions now clearly state whether they connected, missed, or were deflected.
  • Favorite Weapon Text Spoilers Removed a minor text spoiler where a warrior's weapon flavor text would describe a high-efficiency strike before the game engine actually rolled the attack results. This text has been safely moved to display immediately after a confirmed hit lands.
