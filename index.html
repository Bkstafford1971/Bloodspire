🛡️ MAY 29, 2026 — LATEST UPDATE VIEW THE OFFICIAL PLAYTEST ANNOUNCEMENT (5/27)

Official Playtest Proclamation

After many tests and live backend playtesting, I am confident that I am ready to roll Bloodspire Arena out for official playtest. I understand that the first attempts to get this project off the ground were not successful, but I was able to take a step back and work on things more focused. I have run 100's of simulations of the game play mechanics and through some focused live playtesting, I feel very good about the stability and overall game mechanics.

I would like to announce that we will officially start playtest on Wednesday 5/27/2026 at 9:00 PM. I have the upload information that you all gave me a few days ago, and it is cleaned up and ready for the first turn. You do not need to re-upload. If you choose to re-upload with new strategies, you may do so starting at 7:00 PM on Tuesday 5/26/2026. I will be rolling the arena back to turn 0 just prior to that timeframe.

You will need to have the most current Client version prior to any new uploads or first turn download. This new client has numerous bug fixes and enhancements included in it. Please make sure that you install and use Client version 2.7.1.

Some major fixes include:

  • Static stats for new teams. No more canceling and getting a new set of roll-ups
  • No more dead replacements prior to their first turn in the arena
  • Corrected newsletter Arena Happenings report
  • Enhanced fight dialogue lines
  • Special race related abilities
  • Enhanced Scouting report. Each manager will have a dedicated scout now
  • Better archive warrior visibility, including fight visibility for archived warriors
  • Improved error handling for purged files. Files are no longer archived after downloading

Many other updates, fixes and enhancements as well!

I also want to add that I have created a newsletter endpoint as well. In the event that you do not get a newsletter on a download (which sometimes happens if you do not download every turn), you should always receive the fight results, but may only receive the most current newsletter. You can now download any archived newsletter and manually add it to the newsletter directory in your saves folder.

You can access the newsletters at the following link:
http://bloodspirearena.dpdns.org/newsletter
When you access the newsletters from this link, you will have a download button available for any newsletter you need.

Thank you all for sticking with me on this while I ironed out some of the issues that have popped up thus far. For any issues or enhancements, you can utilize the Report an Issue link or post it on the message board.

ARENA UPDATE — MAY 29, 2026

Honored Managers of the Bloodspire Arena,

I have implemented several important balance and quality-of-life improvements based on feedback from the first turn. The following changes are now live:

  • Fixed an exploit related to replacements rollups
  • Modified endurance calculations to prevent early exhaustion
  • Updated permanent injury thresholds — they are now less likely to trigger
  • Cleaned up knockdown logic (warriors were hitting the ground too frequently)
  • Minor verbiage updates and grammar tweaks throughout the client
  • Tweaked the damage formula for all weapons to improve overall balance
  • Adjusted thresholds for light, medium, and heavy damage descriptions
  • Many additional minor cosmetic additions and modifications

You will need to install Client version 2.7.2 for these updates to take full effect.

Going forward, unless a major issue or exploit is discovered, the plan is to release new client versions no more than once per week.

Turn 2 will run on Saturday, May 30th at 9:00 PM CST.

Regular turns will now follow a Tuesday — Thursday — Saturday schedule.

Thank you all again for your patience and valuable feedback. Things are steadily improving and getting much closer to the vision I had when this arena first opened its gates.

— ILNEVAL
